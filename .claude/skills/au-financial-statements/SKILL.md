# Financial statement preparation pack

## Inputs

Entity purpose/type and reporting obligations, agreed framework and reporting period, final trial balance, comparatives, consolidation scope, accounting policies, supporting schedules and relevant subsequent-event/going-concern evidence.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the applicable reporting framework and current effective standards before selecting a statement format. Record the basis for any tier or special-purpose treatment and required approvals.

2. Map every trial-balance account to statements or notes, preserving comparatives and consolidation adjustments. Reconcile unmapped or multiply mapped accounts.

3. Apply documented accounting policies and prepare the relevant presentation/disclosure checklist. Escalate new recognition, measurement, tier or policy choices.

4. Cross-check statements, notes, cash movements and equity roll-forwards to the final workpapers. Record missing disclosures and subsequent-event or going-concern evidence without issuing a compliance or assurance opinion.

## Hand-off and checks

Draft statements, a TB mapping and a disclosure-evidence checklist. A qualified reviewer approves accounting judgements and an authorised human signs and issues.

For each unresolved item record evidence needed, owner, status and next action. Keep dependent results conditional until the item is resolved.
